Xeno-Serum

, also known as the

Flux Gel

, is a viscous substance, which is in virtually every medium and it plays a key role in the

origin

of the

Shadow Adept Shellborne

. It changes the genetic structure of all organisms that come into contact with it, and thus causes a

mutation

.
