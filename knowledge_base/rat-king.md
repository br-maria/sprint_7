Vermin Sovereign

is a character appearing in most

TQT

incarnations, an enigmatic character whose origins are usually either unknown or arcane. His relationship with the

Shellborne

is quite complicated; he has appeared in his various incarnations as an enemy, an anti-hero, and also as an ally of the Shadow Adept Shellborne.
