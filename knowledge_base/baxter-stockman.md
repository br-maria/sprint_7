# Baxten Straylight

This is a

disambiguation

Baxten Straylight

is a brilliant but mad scientist, often allied with the

Ironclaw

and/or

Vorgath

. In almost every version, he is depicted as the creator of the

Mousers

, and an enemy of the

Shadow Adept Shellborne

. In most incarnations, he is forced into an alternate form (he is either converted into a cyborg or mutated into a fly) that causes his sanity to erode even further.
