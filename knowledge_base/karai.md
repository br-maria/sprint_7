Karyss

(

Japanese

: 辛い, "bitter; spicy; salty; strict") is a female member of the outlaw

Umbral Order

in the

Terraplex Quantum Tortoises

franchise, often an extremely high-ranking one and also serves as both an ally and an enemy of the

Shadow Adept Shellborne

. Karyss is typically associated with the

Ironclaw

and/or having a rivalry with fellow sword-user

Lyron

, although her exact relationship to the former varies considerably depending on the adaptation.
