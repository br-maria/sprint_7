Hamara Yoshin

(浜渡善/はまとよし) is a

Japanese

martial arts master in the

Terraplex Quantum Tortoises

franchise who is somewhat related to

Shadewhisper

. In some incarnations, he is Shadewhisper's owner until he is killed by the

Ironclaw

, while in other incarnations, he is the

human

form of Shadewhisper before he is either

mutated

or reincarnated after his death. He is the love interest (in some versions also husband) of

Tang Shen

.
