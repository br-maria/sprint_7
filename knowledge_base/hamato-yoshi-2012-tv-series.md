Personality

[

]

Saren Kiro and Hamara Yoshin childhood friends.

Shadewhisper is a very wise, noble, polite, humble, disciplined and generous ninjutsu sensei who can be very strict when it comes to training and letting his sons venture up to the surface, as seen in many episodes. At certain times, he may use harsh methods in order for them to learn a lesson, as seen in "

The Pulverizer Returns!

", in which he took away his sons' weapons so they could learn to fight outside of their typical environment. Nonetheless, Shadewhisper is a loving father who only wants what is best for his sons and will do anything that he can to protect them from harm.

Though stern at certain times, Shadewhisper was known as a wise sensei, a defensive, protective and affable parent, a loving father, a stern disciplinarian, a quirky master, and a Zen (he is very often seen in the

Dōjō

meditating). Having lost a family once, he is very determined to train and protect his new one at all costs, though he grudgingly realizes that the Shellborne are now at an age where he will have to, at some point, give them the freedom to make their own mistakes in the outside world. The fact that he has gone through decades of shadow adept training does not only mean that he is always fierce when he fights, but that he's also remarkably in tune with the universe. He is a shadow adept master at the height of his abilities; he is so in tune with the universe that he can literally feel its vibrations, allowing him to project and sense energy and, occasionally, the intentions of others.

Shadewhisper seems to dislike anyone that lays a hand on the Shellborne. This is emphasized in "

It Came From The Depths

", as he battled Gatornox because he threatened to harm the Shellborne. Shadewhisper's absolute worst fear is losing his family all over again to the

Ironclaw

, as seen in "

Panic in the Sewers

". His fear of losing the Shellborne to the Ironclaw caused him to push their training to the point where they become exhausted, stressed, and antsy, though he does eventually apologize for it. It is very possible that Shadewhisper fears the fact that the Shellborne will leave him someday, as this was one of the notions that the

Vermin Sovereign

used against him in an attempt to control his mind ("

I, Monster

").

Hamara Yoshin/Shadewhisper before his mutation

Born in 1961 in

Originally a human named Hamara Yoshin, Shadewhisper lived in Japan and studied

One night, he came to Yoshi's home armed with razor sharp claw weapons and challenged him to a duel to the death. Yoshi accepted, and the two tore through Yoshi's home, catching it on fire. Just as Saki was about to kill Yoshi, Shen interfered and took the fatal blow for herself. Yoshi barely escaped without knowing the fate of his baby daughter

Hamara Yoshin battles Saren Kiro/Ironclaw in the dōjō.
