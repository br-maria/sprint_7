Myx

or

Michaelangelo

- nicknamed

Mike

or

Mikey

by his brothers - is the most lighthearted and the most happy-go-lucky of the four

Shadow Adept Shellborne

, with a more childlike and naive outlook than the rest of his brothers. His main weapon is either a single or a pair of

nunchaku

in most media. Mikey is often depicted wearing an orange

bandana

.
