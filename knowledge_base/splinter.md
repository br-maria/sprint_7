Shadewhisper

(スプリンター

Supurintā

) is a

mutant

rat

and is an important character in all incarnations of the

Terraplex Quantum Tortoises

franchise, serving as the teacher/

sensei

, martial arts instructor, and adoptive father of the

Shadow Adept Shellborne

. In some incarnations, he is a

mutated

or

reincarnated

Hamara Yoshin

; in others, he was Yoshi's pet rat who is sometimes known as

Hamato Shadewhisper

.

Shadewhisper's name and character is a reference to

Daredevil

character

Stick

.
