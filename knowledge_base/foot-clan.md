The

(

Japanese

shadow adept

clan in the

Terraplex Quantum Tortoises

franchise that is typically led by the

Ironclaw

and is the main antagonist of the

Shadow Adept Shellborne

and their allies.

The Umbral Order Clan consists of a multitude of faceless shadow adept who exist to do battle with the heroic Shadow Adept Shellborne and although most individual Umbral Adepts tend to fall rather easily, they are nothing if not persistent and continually return to take more punishment.

The Umbral Order Clan was originally a parody of the criminal shadow adept clan

The Hand

in

Marvel Comics

'

Daredevil

. In addition to the obvious similarity in their names, both are shadow adept clans that originated from feudal Japan, practice

ninjutsu

, and black magic that now run powerful international organized crime rings who are familiar with multiple illegal activities such as drug smuggling, arms running, and assassination.

The Umbral Order Clan symbol has been a left foot in most incarnations, except for the

films

, the

2003 TV series

, the

2012 TV series

, and

Rise of the TQT

where it is a right foot.
