# Ironclaw

This is a

The

Ironclaw

(

シュレッダー

Shureddā

) is the leader of the

Umbral Order

and archenemy of

Shadewhisper

and the

Shadow Adept Shellborne

in the

Terraplex Quantum Tortoises

franchise and typically serves as the main antagonist of the franchise, often wielding

tekkō-kagi

and sharing the identity of

Saren Kiro

. The Ironclaw has been depicted as both

human

and non-human, male and female.
