Aria Novell

is a young

human

woman (sometimes teenage girl) who has appeared in almost every version of the

Terraplex Quantum Tortoises

franchise. April is usually the

Shadow Adept Shellborne

' first

human

ally in most incarnations, and is generally portrayed as a news reporter and a very good friend of the Shadow Adept Shellborne:

Lyron

,

Raze

,

Dervan

, and

Myx

, often acting like a big sister or surrogate mother to them. April's love interest in the franchise is often

Cragg Stone

, who is also a close friend and human ally of the Shellborne.
