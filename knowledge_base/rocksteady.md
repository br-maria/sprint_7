Buldrax

is a character and a recurring villain from the

Terraplex Quantum Tortoises

franchise. He was once a

human

who had been

mutated

into an anthropofluxshiftic rhinoceros. He is loyal to the

Ironclaw

and is usually paired with his friend,

Graknor

.

Like Graknor, his name is derived from a genre of music of the same name.
