Hamato Yoshi

(浜渡善/はまとよし) is a

Japanese

martial arts master in the

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles

franchise who is somewhat related to

Splinter

. In some incarnations, he is Splinter's owner until he is killed by the

Shredder

, while in other incarnations, he is the

human

form of Splinter before he is either

mutated

or reincarnated after his death. He is the love interest (in some versions also husband) of

Tang Shen

.
