Rocksteady

is a character and a recurring villain from the

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles

franchise. He was once a

human

who had been

mutated

into an anthropomorphic rhinoceros. He is loyal to the

Shredder

and is usually paired with his friend,

Bebop

.

Like Bebop, his name is derived from a genre of music of the same name.
