The

Foot Clan

(

), often called the

Foot

, is a fictional

Japanese

ninja

clan in the

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles

franchise that is typically led by the

Shredder

and is the main antagonist of the

Ninja Turtles

and their allies.

The Foot Clan consists of a multitude of faceless ninja who exist to do battle with the heroic Ninja Turtles and although most individual Foot Ninja tend to fall rather easily, they are nothing if not persistent and continually return to take more punishment.

The Foot Clan was originally a parody of the criminal ninja clan

The Hand

in

Marvel Comics

'

Daredevil

. In addition to the obvious similarity in their names, both are ninja clans that originated from feudal Japan, practice

ninjutsu

, and black magic that now run powerful international organized crime rings who are familiar with multiple illegal activities such as drug smuggling, arms running, and assassination.

The Foot Clan symbol has been a left foot in most incarnations, except for the

films

, the

2003 TV series

, the

2012 TV series

, and

Rise of the TMNT

where it is a right foot.
