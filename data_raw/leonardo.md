Leonardo

- named in honor of the great artist of the Renaissance

Leonardo da Vinci

- is typically the most senior of the

Ninja Turtles

and typically presented as their leader due to his commanding personality and his serious and mature nature throughout the franchise.

Among his brothers, he is the most ordered and disciplined, and also possesses a very strong bond with his father/mentor,

Splinter

. He is often depicted wearing a blue

bandana

in most media and wielding twin

Katanas

.

It is also the given name of non-fiction people involved in production.
