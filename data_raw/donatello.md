# Donatello

This is a

disambiguation

Donatello

is the

Turtle

that is commonly accepted as the smartest of the group, specifically in matters related to scientific pursuits and technology. He is an accomplished inventor who is able to develop multiple advanced devices with minimal resources, which he devotes to helping both himself and his brothers. In most incarnations, he wears a purple bandana and wields a

Bō

staff.
