Triceratons

are the earliest creation of

Mirage Studios

, pre-dating even

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles

in which they have frequently appeared. In nearly all incarnations, they are an alien race. In most incarnations, the

Triceraton homeworld

was destroyed, and their society lives on

asteroid ships

, often forming the power center of a militaristic

republic

or an

empire

in space.
