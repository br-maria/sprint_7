The

Purple Dragons

are common enemies of the

Ninja Turtles

in several incarnations of the

TMNT franchise

, with their first appearance being in the opening pages of the

very first comic

. They are a

New York

street gang mostly consisting of low-level thugs. They often tend to be connected to the

Foot Clan

in some way.
