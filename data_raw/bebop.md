Bebop

is a character and a recurring villain in the

Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles

franchise. Bebop was once a

human

, who, along with

Rocksteady

, became the

Shredder

's henchmen following his mutation into a

mutant

warthog. He is usually depicted as having a purple mohawk.
